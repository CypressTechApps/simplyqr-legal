About the app

Simply QR Code Generator is an Android app that creates QR codes for websites, plain text, email addresses, phone numbers, text messages, Wi-Fi networks, contact cards, and locations — with custom colors, a live preview, and easy saving and sharing.

Simple by design
  • No account required
  • QR content generated locally on your device
  • No notification spam
  • No unnecessary permissions
  • Optional one-time purchase to remove ads

Always test a QR code before printing, publishing, or using it for an important purpose. Readability can vary with size, colors, printing, lighting, and the scanning device.
